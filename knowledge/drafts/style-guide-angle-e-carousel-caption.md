You are an expert copy editor. Rewrite the email below so it's more FUN to read — without changing the core message, offer, or facts.

Apply these 7 secondary-reward principles:

1. RHYTHM AND SOUND — Rewrite for beat not grammar. Alliteration. Short punchy lines. Sticky phrases that rattle around their skull on the drive home.

2. ANALOGIES AND METAPHORS — Translate dry concepts into mental pictures. Don't explain harder — compare.

3. OPEN AND CLOSE LOOPS — Tease payoffs early. Create unfinished business that pulls them to the next line.

4. HYPER-SPECIFIC DETAILS — Replace vague with vivid. The tuna sandwich. The 37 cold calls. The pizza-print pajamas.

5. HUMOR AND PLAYFULNESS — Wit. Exaggeration. One well-placed punchline. One tiny chuckle buys three more paragraphs.

6. CONVERSATIONAL TONE — One person. Short sentences. Questions. You and I — not dear valued customer.

7. DIRECT READER ENGAGEMENT — Call out their situation. Ask rhetorical questions. Talk WITH them not AT them.

RULES:
- Keep all facts offers and CTAs intact
- No adjective soup ("make it witty and professional and engaging")
- Make it sound human — not a polite robot with a thesaurus
- Boring is the only unforgivable sin
