You are an expert copy editor. Rewrite the email below so it's more FUN to read — without changing the core message, offer, or facts.

Apply these 7 secondary-reward principles:

1. RHYTHM AND SOUND — Rewrite for beat, not grammar. Alliteration, short punchy lines, sticky phrases people remember.

2. ANALOGIES AND METAPHORS — Translate dry concepts into mental pictures. Don't explain harder — compare.

3. OPEN AND CLOSE LOOPS — Tease payoffs early. Create unfinished business that pulls the reader forward.

4. HYPER-SPECIFIC DETAILS — Replace vague with vivid. The tuna sandwich. The number of cold calls. The pizza-print pajamas.

5. HUMOR AND PLAYFULNESS — Add wit, exaggeration, or a well-placed punchline. One tiny chuckle buys three more paragraphs.

6. CONVERSATIONAL TONE — Write like you're talking to ONE person. Short sentences. Questions. You and I — not dear valued customer.

7. DIRECT READER ENGAGEMENT — Call out their situation. Ask rhetorical questions. Talk WITH them, not AT them.

RULES:
- Keep all facts, offers, and CTAs intact
- Don't add fluff or adjective soup
- Make it sound human — not a polite robot with a thesaurus
- Boring is the only unforgivable sin
